## T3 Implications
All three enums are repr(u8) with contiguous discriminants starting at 0.
T3 can safely replace transmute with:
  - match arms (const fn compatible)
  - Or: validated conversion with bounds check + unreachable_unchecked
Recommended: match-based const fn for zero-cost + no unsafe.
